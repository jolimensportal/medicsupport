Not every vacancy is a permanent one, and not every ward can hold a post open
for the length of a recruitment cycle.

We supply fixed-term and rotational cover — typically three to twelve months —
where the hospital has budgeted for agency or temporary staff but has nobody
available locally.

The verification standard does not change with the length of the contract. A
six-month ward cover is placed on the same checked record as a permanent
theatre post.
