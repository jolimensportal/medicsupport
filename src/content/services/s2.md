The service line that decides whether a hospital is safe to come to at night.

  24-hour emergency    Every facility in the group maintains an open casualty
                       department staffed by our own emergency-trained
                       clinicians, not a contracted night service.

  Intensive care        Dedicated critical care units at our tertiary sites,
                       including a 24-bed unit opened in Lagos in 2022 and a
                       30-bed paediatric unit opened in Cebu in 2023.

  Emergency retrieval  Cape Town operates the group's dedicated advanced life
                       support service, with hospital-based response across the
                       Western Cape and retrieval to a MedicSupport facility.

  Trauma and orthopaedics  Dedicated trauma theatres at our Durban site, opened
                       in 2024 to absorb the major trauma volume from the N2
                       corridor.

Casualty cover is the floor, not the ceiling. Where a region has no dependable
emergency service, that gap is usually why we build.
