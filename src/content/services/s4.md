Diagnosis decides outcomes, and our diagnostic capability is deliberately
distributed rather than concentrated.

  Imaging    CT and MRI at our tertiary sites, with ultrasound throughout,
             and a tele-radiology link from Davao back to Metro Manila for
             second-opinion reporting where an on-site radiologist is not
             available.

  Laboratory  Full diagnostic laboratory on site at every facility, including
             pathology, haematology, microbiology and blood banking.

  Cardiac and neuro diagnostics  Cardiac catheterisation at Accra, Lagos,
             Nairobi, Casablanca and Sandton, alongside advanced imaging at our
             most technically equipped site.

  Endoscopy and theatre support  Endoscopy, and the diagnostic and therapeutic
             theatre services that depend on the above.

A referral hospital is only as good as its diagnostics. That is why the imaging
capability to perform a scan is not the same as the capability to read one, and
why we run tele-radiology at all.
