Most recruitment agencies send you a pile of CVs and let you sort it out. That
is not a service.

You send the role, the grade, the destination market and the start date. We
return a shortlist built against exactly that, each entry carrying its verified
credential record and a link to the clinician's public profile so your clinical
or HR lead can check it without an introduction.

Where we cannot fill a role, we say so rather than padding the shortlist.
