This is the part of the business that people find hardest to believe, so it is
worth being precise about what it is and is not.

It is not recruitment. We are not an agency introducing strangers we found. The
clinicians we second abroad are employed directly by MedicSupport. They work in
our own licensed facilities, they hold current registration, and they already
answer to our standards before they go anywhere.

What travels with them:

  The employment relationship   They remain MedicSupport employees. We are
                       responsible for them, not the receiving hospital
                       alone.

  Their verified credential record. Every clinician we employ is checked
                       against their registering body, and the resulting
                       MedicSupport-issued record is publicly verifiable at
                       /verify by anyone, without an account and without
                       asking us.

  Our governance and clinical standard. The same standard applied in Accra is
                       applied in Manchester. Our fellows are trained at our
                       own tertiary sites, not purchased.

Where we place clinicians, we are extending a relationship we already have
rather than creating one from scratch. That is the difference between us and a
broker, and it is the reason a hospital buyer in Europe or America can hold us
to something.

For hospitals and health systems who want to discuss secondment, contact the
group office.
