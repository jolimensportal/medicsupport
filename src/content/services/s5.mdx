A profile is not a CV. It is a record.

Each clinician holds a permanent public address at `/profilename` carrying
their registration number, licence status, verification date, specialisms and
clinical history. An employer can open it and check it without emailing anyone
or asking for a favour.

The clinician controls who sees their contact details. The credential record is
what stays public, because that is what makes the network trustworthy.

We will not publish a credential we have not confirmed. An unverified profile
exists but is labelled unverified, which is the honest state for it.
