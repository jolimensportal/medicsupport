Maternity is the service most of our sites are built around, and the reason
several of them exist.

  Antenatal and postnatal  Continuous care from booking through delivery and
                           beyond, at every site with a maternity unit.

  Neonatal intensive care   Dedicated neonatal units at our tertiary facilities,
                           including the 30-bed paediatric intensive care unit
                           at Mandaue and neonatal cover at Adenta.

  Complex referral          Our tertiary sites in Accra, Lagos and Nairobi
                           accept obstetric and gynaecological referrals that
                           cannot be managed at secondary level.

  Safe delivery             Reducing avoidable maternal and neonatal mortality
                           is the single clinical objective our facilities are
                           commissioned against, and it is measured per site.

Gynaecological surgery — including laparoscopic procedures — is available at
our tertiary and larger secondary sites.
