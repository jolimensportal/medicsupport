This is the part that decides whether a placement is real.

Registration is verified directly against the issuing nursing or medical
council, not against a CV screenshot. Licence status, restrictions, and any
disciplinary history are checked. Identity documents and the right to work are
confirmed.

We then map that record onto what the destination regulator actually requires,
which varies by country and by profession, and is the step most informal
recruiters skip or get wrong.

Where a clinician needs additional examination, registration or language
assessment before practising, we tell both sides what is required and what it
takes.
