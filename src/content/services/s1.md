Placement is the core of the business and the reason the rest exists.

A hospital in Dublin, Dubai or Detroit tells us the role, the grade and the
start date. We return clinicians who hold current registration in their home
country, whose licence maps onto the destination regulator's requirements, and
whose identity and right to work we have confirmed.

The hospital makes the hiring decision and employs the clinician directly. We
are paid by the employer for the placement, and we say so plainly rather than
letting it look like a favour to the nurse.

What we do not do: we do not represent a vacancy we cannot verify, and we do
not present an unverified clinician as verified.
