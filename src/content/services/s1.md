This is the core of MedicSupport, and everything else sits on top of it.

We own and operate the facilities. Each is licensed by the relevant authority in
its own country, staffed by clinicians we employ directly, and run under a
single clinical and governance standard across the group rather than eighteen
separate local standards.

Our sites are not uniform. They are sized to the population they serve:

  Tertiary   major referral centres taking complex work from a region or
             country beyond their own catchment — cardiac, obstetric and
             oncological referrals, advanced diagnostics.

  Secondary  regional hospitals covering a city and its hinterland, with
             inpatient, theatre, maternity and outpatient provision.

  Community  local facilities addressing a documented gap in secondary or
             inpatient access, often the most recent thing we opened in a
             country.

Every site runs 24-hour emergency cover, because a hospital that closes its
casualty department overnight is not a hospital in any sense our clinicians
would recognise.
