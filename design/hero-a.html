We run hospitals. And we staff them with our own people.

MedicSupport Network has operated hospitals and clinics across Africa and Asia for 18 years — 18 facilities, 6 countries, 3,950 licensed beds. Our doctors, nurses, midwives and paramedics work in our hospitals. Some are seconded to hospitals and health systems in Europe and America.
